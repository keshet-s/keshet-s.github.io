What is a Design Partnership?

This isn't beta testing. It's a genuine collaboration where you help shape what we build.

What This Means:

  • You run a live cohort through one module using the platform — 1 to 2 weeks, with real students doing real exercises.
  • We handle setup, integration, and support throughout.
  • You tell us what works, what doesn't, and what's missing.
  • From there, we talk about what an ongoing relationship looks like.

This is not a beta test where you report bugs into a form. It's a direct collaboration.

The Problem

Instructor time going to the wrong places

Marking exercises, repeating the same lab support, writing the same feedback on the same mistakes. The work that matters most, being present for the student who needs you, keeps getting displaced.

No visibility into what's happening during a lab session

By the time a student flags they're stuck, the productive intervention window has often already closed. The LMS shows green. Something else is happening in the terminal.

What Adaptly Does

Students get help when they need it

The AI tutor detects when a student is stuck during a live lab exercise and intervenes with contextual guidance — without handing them the answer.

You have real insights

The instructor dashboard shows you what every student is actually doing in real time — not just what they submitted at the end.

Integrates with your existing LMS via LTI/Moodle. No rip-and-replace.

What We're Looking For

Ideal Partner:

  • Runs hands-on, lab-based cybersecurity training (instructor-led cohorts or certification programmes)
  • Students do real terminal work during exercises — not just watching demos or answering MCQs
  • Has at least one module coming up in the next 60–90 days that could run as a pilot
  • Willing to give honest feedback during and after the pilot — good and bad
  • Comfortable with early-stage software: not everything will be perfect, and that's the point

Not Right If:

  • ✗ You run pure theory, lecture-based, or video-only training with no hands-on lab component
  • ✗ You need a fully polished enterprise product before you'll consider it
  • ✗ You can't commit to running one real cohort through it and giving feedback afterward

Common Questions

Q: What if it doesn't work for us?

A: You can exit during the partnership phase. We're looking for the right fit, not just any customer.

Q: What does the pilot actually look like?

A: We pick one module from your existing schedule — 1 to 2 weeks, one live cohort, real exercises. We handle setup and integration. You run it as normal. We both watch what happens and debrief at the end.

Q: What does it cost?

A: There's a flat pilot fee — we discuss the amount based on your module setup. It's designed to be accessible, not a big commercial commitment. If the pilot works, we agree on ongoing pricing together.

Q: Do we own our content?

A: Completely. You can export anytime.

Q: What tools and environments do you support?

A: We have native support for common terminal-based lab tools. If your module uses something outside that, there's a one-time setup involved — we'll flag it upfront before you commit to anything.

If you run cybersecurity training where students do real hands-on lab work — and you recognise the problems on this page — we'd like to have a conversation. Not a pitch. A genuine conversation about whether running one pilot module together makes sense for both of us.
