The Bottleneck

You've Built a Program That Works.
Now It's Hitting a Ceiling.

Your cohorts run at 25–50 students. Your labs are rigorous. Your pass rates matter. Your reputation is everything. But growth keeps running into the same constraint: instructor dependency.

More Live Lab Troubleshooting

Every new cohort demands more real-time hands-on support from your senior team.

More Senior Instructor Hours

Your most valuable people are stretched thin - and hiring more is expensive and slow.

More Operational Complexity

Coordinating labs, feedback loops, and troubleshooting at scale creates compounding overhead.

More Fixed Costs

Adding cohorts means adding headcount. Margins don't improve - they flatten.

The uncomfortable question:
Is your delivery model built for outcomes - or built for scale? Because right now, it's hard to optimize for both. Scaling this way doesn't compound. It stretches.

Why This Roundtable Exists

A Small, Closed-Door Strategy Discussion

We're hosting a private session - 8–10 operators max - to explore one central question: what would need to change in lab-heavy certification prep to scale without increasing instructor headcount?

This isn't a webinar. It's a working session with founders and Heads of Training who already run structured cybersecurity certification programs, care deeply about pass rates, feel instructor bandwidth as their primary growth bottleneck, and want a smarter delivery model.

We're not discussing theory. We're exploring structural redesign.

This room is for people who:

  • Already run structured cybersecurity certification programs
  • Care deeply about pass rates and program outcomes
  • Feel instructor bandwidth as their primary growth bottleneck
  • Want a smarter, more scalable delivery model
  • Are open to rethinking how their program is structured
What We'll Dive Into

Session Agenda

Topic 01

Growth Bottlenecks

Where instructor time is actually consumed - and where it doesn't need to be. An honest audit of your delivery model's true constraints.

Instructor Scalability

Lab-Heavy Delivery Models

A working discussion on the specific levers available to operators willing to redesign their delivery model for scale.

Curriculum flow Lab support architecture Cohort structure Feedback systems Student troubleshooting pathways

  • A clearer picture of where the real friction in lab-heavy delivery actually sits
  • An honest read on what would need to change first — and what others have already tried

Why This Is Small

And Why That Matters

We're keeping this to 8–10 people because the format only works when everyone in the room can speak.

1

The conversation will go deep. Real numbers and real constraints will be discussed. That requires a room small enough for honesty.

2

This is for decision-makers only. Every seat needs to be occupied by someone with the authority - and the context - to actually change how their program operates.

3

The format doesn't work at scale. A working session with 40 people becomes a panel. We're not running a panel.

More importantly: this session is also exploratory.

We are actively working on new ways to reduce instructor dependency in lab-heavy environments - and we want to build it alongside a small group of serious operators.

Some participants may be invited to collaborate further as early design partners.

Not everyone. Only those aligned.
