The problem

                
One instructor. Twelve terminals.
No idea who's drowning.

                
In a hands-on lab, the students who ask for help are rarely the ones who need it most. The struggling ones go quiet — retrying the same command, staring at the same error — and you find out after the session ends, when the window to intervene has already closed.

            

            

                

                    
12+

                    
students per instructor in a typical cert cohort

                

                

                    
0

                    
signal from the students who go quiet

                

                

                    
Too late

                    
when "lab completed: no" finally tells you something was wrong

                

            

            
        

    


    
    

        

            

                
How it works

                
From silent struggle to timely help, in three steps

            

            

                

                    
1

                    
The agent watches the work

                    
A lightweight desktop agent reads the signal in the work itself: commands run, errors hit, time gone idle. No screen recordings to scrub through — just live activity from every lab machine.

                    

                        terminal commands
                        error output
                        idle time
                    

                

                

                    
2

                    
Stuck detection fires

                    
Repeated failures. A long idle right after an error. Circling the same dead end. The moment a student crosses the stuck threshold, they're flagged — in real time, not in the post-lab report.

                    

                        ⚠ flagged in seconds
                    

                

                

                    
3

                    
Hints escalate. You see everything.

                    
The AI Tutor opens with a conceptual nudge and only escalates if the student stays stuck — while the instructor dashboard shows every flag, every hint, every screen's activity.

Integration

                    
Plugs into the stack you already run

                    
Adaptly doesn't replace your LMS or your lab environments — it adds the layer that's missing between them. The instructor dashboard drops into Moodle via LTI 1.3, and the agent runs alongside the tooling your students already use.

                    
    
                        
  • Moodle via LTI 1.3 — the dashboard lives where your instructors already work

  • Your existing labs — the agent observes; it doesn't ask you to migrate exercises

  • No workflow replacement — keep your curriculum, grading, and enrolment as they are
