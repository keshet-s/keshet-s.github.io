Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 2025

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our site.

Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Required for the website to function. These cookies cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

Google Analytics (_ga, _gid)

  • Purpose: Understand how visitors use our site
  • Duration: _ga — 14 months · _gid — 24 hours
  • Third-party: Yes (Google)

Preference Cookies

Cookie Consent (cc_cookie)

  • Purpose: Remember your cookie preferences
  • Duration: 6 months

Managing Cookies

You can control which cookies are stored on your device through the following options:

  • Our cookie banner when you first visit the site
  • Your browser's built-in cookie settings
  • Google Analytics Opt-out →
